The engraver

One bench. One set of hands.

Jason Carlin is a designer and hand engraver who has spent over 27 years cutting patterns into metal. He has engraved everything from musical instruments to heirloom weapons, but the work he is known for is the ring you wear every day: wedding bands and engagement rings.

His designs have been sold by some of the largest names in American jewelry. At his shop inside the Woodbridge Jewelry Exchange, he works directly with you, from the first sketch to the last polish.
